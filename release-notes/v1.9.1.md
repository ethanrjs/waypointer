Small follow-up fixes for the 1.9.0 route catalog.

## Fixed

* Route rows in the catalog no longer cut off the install count. The stats column now reads `149wp - 0 installs` instead of truncating mid-word.
* The zone filter's arrow now points up while the dropdown is open.
* Publishing a dungeon route now says clearly: dungeon routes cannot be published yet. We will add that later. Previously the publish form showed a confusing "Join a SkyBlock island first." message for dungeon room routes, and Catacombs floor routes could be published even though they can't work in randomly generated dungeons.
