# Waypointer 1.10.0

## Added

- Crystal Hollows structure detection and automatic sharing with other Waypointer users.
- Wishing Compass solver and Crystal Nucleus waypoints.
- Fast metal detector solver
- A Structures folder and dedicated mining settings.
- Short published route codes and links.
- ChunkLogger route imports.
- Backward route progression when retracing your steps.
- Separate colors for previous, current, and upcoming waypoints, with smoother transitions between them.
- Optional smoothing for waypoint outlines, route lines, and tracers.
- More control over waypoint labels, including opacity and camera effects.
- A confirmation screen before importing configuration changes.
- A contributor badge and custom color for acidangels.

## Changed

- The new waypoint renderer is now enabled by default.
- All new Waypointer share codes use the WP: prefix.
- Your publisher identity now carries across launcher profiles.
- Coordinates-only exports are now the default, with clearer controls for choosing what to export.
- Etherwarp notification sounds are configurable.
- Waypoint Painter and 3D route previews are grouped under Extras settings.
- Clearer settings navigation, import feedback, and export previews.
- Publishing warns when custom waypoint painting will not be included.
- Translation fixes and updated UI checkmarks.

## Fixed

- SecretRoutes imports and incorrect Mineshaft targeting.
- Tracers becoming unexpectedly thin.
- Route-specific Paint appearance now takes priority over the global appearance setting.
- Focused temporary waypoint beacons stay visible when beacon mode is Off.
- Improved image import compatibility and painter behavior.
- Open Editor now opens the route you just imported.
- The route catalog resumes loading correctly after returning from another screen.
- Temporary and generated routes stay separate from saved folders during edits, copies, and bulk actions.
- Safer recovery from damaged configuration files.
- Turning off structure sharing or letting shared locations expire no longer loses your own discoveries.

## Performance

- Much faster rendering for waypoint clusters.
- More responsive route exports, publishing, and large-route previews.
- Faster paint previews and fewer repeated texture uploads.
- Less background traffic for structure sharing: 80% fewer routine snapshots under normal conditions, while new discoveries still share immediately.
