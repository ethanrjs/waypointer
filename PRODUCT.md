# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hypixel SkyBlock players managing routes and waypoints while the game remains visible behind the interface. They need to find, organize, show, hide, import, export, and edit routes quickly without leaving their gameplay context.

## Product Purpose

Waypointer aims to be the best possible waypoint manager for Hypixel SkyBlock. Success means route management is fast and predictable across islands and dungeon rooms, while the interface stays compact enough to use in-game.

## Brand Personality

Clean, restrained, dependable. The UI should feel like a focused Minecraft utility: familiar at first glance, deliberate in its details, and quieter than the world behind it.

## Anti-references

Avoid opaque modal-heavy flows, stacked cards, decorative effects that compete with gameplay, mismatched vanilla and custom controls, and unfamiliar interactions that hide standard route-management actions.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the player in context by preserving the visible game world behind one translucent interface layer.
2. Use spacing and alignment for hierarchy before weight or color.
3. Reserve the cyan accent for selection, focus, and meaningful state.
4. Keep frequent route actions obvious, compact, and consistent across screens.
5. Prefer familiar controls and direct manipulation over extra dialogs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast over varied world backgrounds, visible hover and keyboard-focus states, full keyboard navigation, and labels or shapes alongside color for important state. Keep muted content legible and avoid decorative motion.
